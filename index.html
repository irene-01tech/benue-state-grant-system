About the system

A single source of truth for menstrual health grants

Menstrual poverty keeps girls out of class across secondary schools — but the resources meant to fix that often move through disconnected, undocumented channels. This platform gives every NGO, Government body, Private Organisation, and school a shared, accountable system to request, fund, deliver, and verify support.

Built as a final-year Computer Science project, the system digitises the entire grant lifecycle: from a school's first needs assessment through to photographic proof that hygiene kits reached students — with every step timestamped and attributable.

Objectives

What this system is built to achieve

Every feature exists to serve one of these five goals.

1

Centralise grant administration

Replace scattered spreadsheets and phone calls with one shared record for every organisation.

2

Verify every organisation

Ensure only legitimate schools, NGOs, Government bodies, and private organisations can request or disburse resources.

3

Match resources to real need

Use structured needs assessments so grants target the schools that need them most.

4

Prove distribution happened

Require photographic and documentary evidence before a distribution is marked verified.

5

Measure real impact

Capture outcomes through impact surveys, not just delivery confirmations.

Who it's for

Built around four kinds of users

Every account is reviewed before it goes live, so trust is built into the platform from day one.

Super Administrator

Oversees the entire platform — approvals, users, reports, audit trail, and system configuration.

  • Approves or rejects every registration
  • Full visibility over every transaction
  • Manages users, settings & announcements

NGO / Government Organisations

Create grant programmes, review needs assessments and applications from schools, allocate resources, and verify that support reaches students.

  • Create & manage grant programmes
  • Approve, reject & allocate resources
  • Also review School & Private Org registrations

Private Organisations / Individuals

Fund and manage smaller-scale grant programmes with the same transparent workflow used across the platform.

  • Create grant programmes at your own scale
  • Review & approve applications from schools
  • Set your own stated reach estimate

Secondary Schools

Register, complete a needs assessment, and apply directly for the resources your students need.

  • Needs assessment & grant applications
  • Track application status in real time
  • Upload distribution evidence & photos
